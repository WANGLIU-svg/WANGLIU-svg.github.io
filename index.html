ABOUT ME

Where education
meets exploration.

An educator with a passion for sports and photography.

My work sits at the intersection of education, movement and visual storytelling. With more than two decades in sports education, I believe that curiosity, discipline and the willingness to explore are essential parts of a meaningful life.

Away from teaching, I capture moments through photography and explore the underwater world as an Advanced Open Water Diver. These experiences continually shape how I observe people, places and the world around me.
